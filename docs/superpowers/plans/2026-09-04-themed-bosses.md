# Themed boss implementation plan

Date: 2026-09-04. Status: planning only.

Spec: [Three themed bosses](../specs/2026-09-04-themed-bosses-design.md).

## Integration boundary

All three bosses are in scope. One owner integrates `run.gd`, terrain generation and the manifest; music hooks use these same AI functions and must be serialized at that mutation boundary. Sentinel's arena-0 anchors integrate after spawner/zone placement and before final geometry validation. Modifier votes may scale a boss, never change its subnet identity. Independent shader/audio/data work may proceed against agreed IDs; workers skip builds, tests, formatters and linters until source edits settle.

Use LSP references before modifying exported symbols. Source lines in the spec are navigation hints, not an exhaustive reference list. Do not create compatibility aliases, temporary plain-boss stubs or intermediate commits that claim the three encounters work before their behavior exists.

## Steps

1. **Replace positional boss identity end-to-end.** In `data/enemy_table.gd`, replace the old `ice` identity with `root_cause`, add `sentinel_array` and `worm_exe_segment`, and expose a validated subnet-to-ID/index lookup. Cache resolved indices for run use without rebuilding the registry per tick. Required IDs cannot fall back to a daemon. Remove `EnemyTable.ICE` and migrate its runtime consumers in `spawn_director.gd`/`run.gd`: spawning, threat/presence, arrival/flip guards, boss pathing, death/clear, straggler handling and rendering. Distinguish boss-type membership from terminal boss death; a worm body must not clear the subnet. Migrate dependent fixture setup through real per-boss spawn paths. Update `shaders/glyph.gdshader` for the new glyphs without image assets.
2. **Implement Sentinel Array with real capture.** In `terrain.gd`, reserve four fixed distant arena-0 anchors, safe pads and player-width connections. Update collision, zones, visual rectangles and derived caches coherently; explicitly reject invalid exported layouts rather than rely only on assertions. In `run.gd`, spawn the core with the existing board-clear/arrival contract, initialize four uncaptured spires, and add the ordered boss step. For each uncaptured spire, any LIVE occupant fills progress at the fixed rate; no occupants drain it; clamp and latch capture permanently. No four-times rate when four players occupy one spire. Guard damage/corruption admission while shielded, including the direct zone path as well as grid-mediated attacks. Keep the recommended core stationary while shielded and spires inert. Exposure and grid membership change consistently on the next world tick after the fourth capture, once, with a visible/audible activation cue.
3. **Implement Worm.exe and Root Cause completely.** Spawn Worm.exe as eight real entities, one head at 550 base HP plus seven bodies at 80 base HP, scaled once. Track positive adjudicated damage, a 12-second recommended interval and four-event budget. On a damage-free interval, regrow below ten total live segments (including head), otherwise heal the head by up to 15% scaled max; spend at most one event per interval and stop permanently at budget zero. Regrown segment index is the live maximum plus one, never live count; with at most four regrows the maximum remains 11 and trail lookups remain safe. Head death cascades remaining bodies without multiple clear/bank calls. For Root Cause, derive the three phases from current HP fraction, skip directly across thresholds when appropriate, reset inherited AI/submerge/velocity on change and implement actual ambush, telegraphed three-shot barrage and charge behavior. Lock Root Cause's windup direction through dash launch without changing ordinary chargers' late-targeting contract. Reuse charge machinery with an explicit caller-selected policy where suitable; don't add an unrequested global charger rebalance. Keep ensemble emission at real attack transitions and successful shot creation.
4. **Integrate lifecycle, recovery and presentation.** Classify every new simulation field in `STATE_FIELDS`, keep presentation flash/fades and derived boss-row/terrain-anchor caches in `NOT_IN_MANIFEST`, and maintain every per-entity field at spawn plus both swap-remove sites. Add transactional snapshot validation using the whole incoming payload: bounded finite counters/timers, spire progress/flags/count consistency, unique safe worm indices and valid encounter lifecycle. Restore active encounters and presentation safely without replaying capture/reward events. In `run.gd`/`ui.gd`, render distinct boss forms, objective/health state, capture progress, regenerative changes and honest attack warnings; use text/shape as well as color. Add real synthesized arrival/death/capture/regen/phase sounds to `synth.gd`, removing obsolete unused ICE IDs if the cutover makes them unreachable. Keep `test_audio_events` bank coverage aware of any new indirect lookup; no silent unknown sound IDs.
5. **Verify after the integrated source settles, then finish.** Update existing substantive campaign/gate/collapse/ending/flow/manifest/multiplayer tests for real boss identity; delete obsolete last-row pins instead of replacing them with static table-length assertions. Add focused behavior regressions only for shield bypass, sticky capture/one-time clear, body-vs-head death, regen budget/index boundary, phase skip/warning accuracy and transactional recovery. Run `tools/run_tests.sh` with UDP permitted and inspect the runner's script-error and performance verdicts; a hand-run suite saying PASS is not independent proof. Exercise all three real encounters windowed, in solo and co-op, including weak-damage builds, simultaneous captures, head targeting, all regeneration paths and resync during each encounter. Confirm the first two clears reach the shared teleporter vote and the last ends the run. Observe warnings, banners, glyphs and sound; record fight durations/damage taken, not an analytic promise of balance. After successful smoke, remove disposable drivers/UIDs and update maintained documentation/manual, including retirement of the ICE-last invariant. Do not hand-edit generated codemaps or commit/push without instruction.

## Required observations

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Shielded Sentinel on hazard, hit by all relevant attack paths | No damage/corruption bypass; capture, not attacking spires, releases the core |
| Several players on one versus four spires | One-spire fill rate stays fixed; split party progresses independently; captured spires never drain |
| Worm body dies, tail slot relocates, then regrows | Boss remains active, live indices stay unique and trail-safe; only head death clears |
| Four regen opportunities with cap reached | Both body-regrow and head-heal paths operate within one shared budget; full-health healing never exceeds max |
| Root Cause crosses two phase thresholds in one hit | Exactly one transition to the correct phase; no stale submerge/velocity; dash follows the displayed direction |

Recovery checks must compare subsequent real simulation outcomes and hashes, not merely field copies. Performance differences from larger segmented bosses require measurement and written explanation; they do not authorize reducing the approved three-boss scope or silently changing global difficulty.
